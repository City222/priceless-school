Our History & Vision

Established in Akwakuma, Owerri North, Imo State, Priceless Child International Standard & Computer Based School was founded with a passion for integrating modern digital literacy with core academic standards. What began as a vision to revolutionize primary and secondary education in Imo State has grown into a premier institution pioneering Computer Based Testing (CBT), interactive ICT learning, and comprehensive academic development.


Our curriculum blends state educational guidelines with modern technology, ensuring every student develops strong digital competency alongside academic excellence, moral discipline, and leadership values.
