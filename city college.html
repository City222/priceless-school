Principal's Welcome Note

Welcome to City College! Our school offers a serene environment tailored for holistic student growth. We believe that true education reaches beyond the textbook — it cultivates curiosity, integrity, and resilience.

Through state-of-the-art facilities, modern digital learning tools, and a curriculum aligned with global standards, we ensure every child receives the guidance necessary to thrive in an evolving world.

Our Mission

To provide quality and holistic education through dedicated teaching, moral guidance, and a supportive learning environment that empowers students to develop their full potential and thrive in a changing world.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution that nurtures disciplined, confident, and innovative learners equipped to excel academically and contribute positively to society.
